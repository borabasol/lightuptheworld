Light Up the World — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Light Up the World ("the game") is a geography game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics, and it does not access your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (places found, energy, daily streak and settings) is saved only on your own device. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. You can also reset it from the game's settings.

Night photo and sharing

If you choose to take a "night photo" or copy your Daily result, the image or text is created on your device. It leaves your device only if you decide to share it through another app you pick, and that app's own privacy policy then applies.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children under 13 either.

Advertising

The current version shows no third-party ads and contains no advertising SDK. If ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and any ad partner will follow Google Play's Families policy.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
